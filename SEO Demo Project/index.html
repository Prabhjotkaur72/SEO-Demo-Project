What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process of improving a website so that it can appear more effectively in search engine results and attract relevant organic traffic.

If you want to learn more, check our SEO types section to understand different areas of SEO.

Types of SEO

On-Page SEO

On-page SEO focuses on optimizing content, headings, title tags, meta descriptions, internal links and other elements on a webpage.

Off-Page SEO

Off-page SEO focuses on activities outside the website, such as building quality backlinks and improving online authority.

Technical SEO

Technical SEO focuses on crawling, indexing, website speed, mobile responsiveness, sitemap and other technical aspects of a website.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take?

SEO usually takes time because rankings depend on competition, website quality, content and many other factors.

Is SEO free?

Organic SEO does not require paying Google for each click, although businesses may invest in tools, content and SEO services.
